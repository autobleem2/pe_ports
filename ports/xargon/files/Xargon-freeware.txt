Xargon - freeware notice
========================

Xargon (1994) was published by Epic MegaGames. Its author, Allen Pilgrim, owns the copyright and released the
entire Xargon trilogy as freeware on 4 August 2008. His public statement, as quoted in the article
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xargon:

    "I own the copyright for the game and have released the entire trilogy as freeware."

The same release put the source code of Kiloblaster and Xargon under a licence that lets anyone "use this in any way
they choose" (Kiloblaster_and_Xargon_Freeware_License.txt, https://github.com/dos-games/vanilla-kiloblaster).

The game's files in the XARGON/ folder are the registered v3.0 trilogy exactly as the RGB Classic Games site
(https://classicdosgames.com/game/Xargon.html) hands them out: nothing in them was changed, added or removed.
LICENSE.DOC and FILE_ID.DIZ in that folder are the 1994 texts ("not shareware") from before the declaration of 2008;
they are left as they were. The game stays its author's.
